About

I work at the intersection of strong statistical fundamentals and shippable products. My background is theory first, built on probability, regression, variance analysis, and linear algebra. I care just as much, though, about whether the final thing is deployed, reproducible, and understandable to a non-technical person.

shoot_here.py

Shoot Here — Photography Hotspot Recommender

Flagship · Solo
Recommendation engine · Jul 2026
  • Built an end-to-end engine over 264K geotagged photos, engineering a pipeline that ingests via the Flickr API, clusters photos into 483 ranked locations (DBSCAN, haversine), and enriches them through the Google Places API — all cached for full reproducibility.
  • Designed a composite "photogenicity" scoring model and revised it three times from evidence — e.g. replacing raw photo count with unique-photographer count after single-user clusters gamed the rankings — plus a baseline-normalized time-of-day fit that surfaces golden-hour specialists.
  • Shipped a deployed multi-page app backed by a SQLite database serving live parameterized ranking queries, translating model outputs into plain-language recommendations for a non-technical user.
